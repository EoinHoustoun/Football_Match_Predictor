# GW5 review, 22 Sep 2026

Main lost £20,077 on GW5 (1 of 7 draws), Mock Two lost £15,269 (0 of 7).
Both lines had the full 50% pending cap riding. This note records what was
checked, what was variance, and what was not.

## What was variance

- Model expected 2.4 draws from Main's 7; P(<= 1) = 0.24. Not a tail event.
- Over all 27 Main bets the model expected 9.9 wins and got 10.
- League draw rate this season is 32% (16 of 50) against a 22% long run
  (2021-25, 760 matches), so a cold week was overdue.

## What was not variance

### 1. Bet volume is 3-4x any backtest season

| | bets per gameweek |
|---|---|
| backtest 2023-24 (live config, honest calibrator) | 1.5 |
| backtest 2024-25 | 2.1 |
| backtest 2025-26 | 2.5 |
| backtest on 2026-27's own first 40 matches | 3 bets total (all promoted-side longshots) |
| **live 2026-27** | **5.4** (27 bets in 5 gameweeks) |

Placed prices are not the cause: live odds average 1.00x the football-data
best-of-panel price for the same matches.

### 2. The cause is the live 40-week isotonic calibrator

`cached_calibrators` fits on the trailing 40-week backtest. That window is
now 15 Dec 2025 to 20 Sep 2026, 271 matches, draw rate 32.1%. The fitted
map is:

| raw draw prob | live calibrated |
|---|---|
| 0.18-0.20 | 0.240 |
| 0.22 | 0.286 |
| 0.25-0.36 | 0.342 (flat plateau) |
| 0.40 | 0.450 (cap) |

On the 15 bet matches that appear in the honest pack, the pack's raw draw
probability averaged 0.249 and live bet at 0.308. Examples: Liverpool v
Fulham raw 0.205 -> live 0.346; Hull v Man United raw 0.209 -> live 0.355;
Sunderland v Arsenal raw 0.229 -> live 0.408.

Long-run evidence for the raw 0.22-0.30 band (pooled 2023-26, 3 seasons,
honest calibration, `draw_calibration_20260916_221533.json`): actual draw
rate 0.234-0.241, Pinnacle implied 0.225-0.256, flat ROI -1% to -6%. The
edge historically lives in raw >= 0.30 (actual 0.26-0.31 vs Pinnacle
0.25-0.27, flat ROI +18% to +42%, n = 217).

The 16 Sep calibration choice (`validate_draw_calibration_20260916`) kept
the 40-week isotonic on total profit £33.9k, but that total is one season:
2025-26 +£43.9k, 2023-24 -£3.7k, 2024-25 -£6.3k, and its out-of-sample log
loss (0.575) was the worst of the four options, worse than no calibration
(0.562). This is the £100k artifact pattern in a milder form: a calibrator
fitted on a draw-heavy window inflating the volume and size of bets in the
band where the model has no edge.

This season's 22 draw bets went 10/22 (45%), which is consistent with the
freak window continuing, not with the long run. Both readings are live at
once; the sample cannot separate them yet.

### 3. The pending cap binds live but never in backtest

`validate_cap_allocation.py` (5-season pack, live config, no pending-count
limit): at 50% and 40% the cap bound in 0 gameweeks across 194 bets; at
30% it bound in 14-24. Live it bound in GW5 on both lines. Per the
validation rules that means an input differs, and the input is (2) above:
backtest volume is 1.5-2.5 bets per gameweek, live is 5.4.

Consequences:
- Pro-rata versus truncate allocation cannot be tested on history, because
  the cap never engages there. Only at 30% do they differ, and pro-rata was
  slightly better on every headline (worst season -8,736 vs -8,818, max DD
  88.3% vs 89.1%, compounded £951 vs £756). Too few binding gameweeks to
  call it a region.
- The Main (1.0 Kelly) vs Mock Two (0.45 Kelly) A/B is confounded whenever
  the cap binds: both lines then stake exactly 50% of bankroll.

The edge indicator is still positive against the exchange, but roughly half
the displayed figure, and not comparable with last season's -0.83% vs

## Decisions applied, 22 Sep afternoon

Eoin delegated the call. Applied, with the tightening-only rule as the test:

1. **Raw draw-probability floor 0.30, both lines.** `scripts/validate_raw_floor.py`
   on the three-season pack, gameweek mode, live config, no pending-count limit:

   | calibration | floor | worst season | max DD | bets/GW | CLV | £10k compounded |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | isotonic 40w (live) | none | -6,340 | 83.1% | 2.08 | +2.97% | 12,449 |
   | isotonic 40w (live) | 0.28 | -4,865 | 65.9% | 1.56 | +2.52% | 39,902 |
   | isotonic 40w (live) | **0.30** | **-2,127** | **65.8%** | 1.48 | +2.52% | **80,252** |
   | isotonic 40w (live) | 0.32 | -4,927 | 67.5% | 1.32 | +2.43% | 21,453 |
   | none | none | -8,844 | 88.4% | 1.99 | +3.25% | 3,058 |
   | none | 0.30 | -7,428 | 82.9% | 1.54 | +3.44% | 25,735 |

   Every floor from 0.28 to 0.32 beats no floor on worst season and drawdown
   under both regimes, so this is a region. 0.30 is also the peak on
   compounded return, which is the one number here to hold loosely.
   Settings key `min_raw_draw_prob`, slider in both settings panels, Pre-Flight
   row, skip reason `raw_floor` in activity.log with the raw, calibrated and
   EV numbers. Backups `*.20260922_165016.preRawFloor.json`.
2. **Removed the second auto-bet loop from both portfolio tabs.** It held no
   lock, logged nothing, skipped the no-history gate and read fixtures 30 days
   out where the shared run reads 7. At 12:08 today it placed three Main bets
   on 10-11 Oct fixtures (£11,273) seconds after the shared run logged "No
   upcoming fixtures". Those bets stay (bets are not removed); two of the
   three have calibrated probabilities that imply a raw number under the floor.
3. **CLV against the exchange close** when Pinnacle is absent: `load_data`
   keeps `BFEC*`, and they sit second in the closing-odds chain. This
   season's bets recomputed: Main median 7.8% to 3.9% (22 of 27 positive),
   Mock Two 7.7% to 3.95%. Backups `*.preClvRecompute.json`.

Not shipped: pro-rata cap allocation (simulator only), calibrator window
change (Mock Two research first), Mock Two cap scaling.

## Options considered

1. **Raw-probability floor of 0.30 on draws** (tightening). Backed by the
   pooled 3-season band table. Would have removed 11 of the 15 matched live
   bets this season, of which 5 won. Needs a gameweek backtest with the
   live-style calibrator before sign-off.
2. **Calibrator window of 2 years, or none** for live sizing. The 16 Sep
   run showed 2y isotonic and logistic both lose money in total; "none" has
   the best log loss after logistic. This is a calibration-method change,
   so sandbox in Mock Two first.
3. **Cap allocation pro-rata** (`exposure_cap_mode="prorata"`, now in the
   simulator). Neutral to slightly positive on history; the real case for
   it is live GW5, where placement order decided who got £7,298 and who got
   £390. Reallocation, needs sign-off.
4. **Scale Mock Two's cap with its Kelly fraction** so the A/B measures
